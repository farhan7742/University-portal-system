Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

To reset your password, go to the login page and click on "Forgot Password". Enter your registered email address and follow the instructions sent to your email. If you don't receive the email within 5 minutes, check your spam folder.

How can I enroll in a course?

Navigate to the "Course List" page from the sidebar, find the course you want to enroll in, and click the "Enroll" button. Some courses may require instructor approval or have prerequisites that need to be met first.

How do I submit assignments?

Go to your enrolled course page, find the assignment section, and click on the assignment you want to submit. You can upload files, write text responses, or submit links depending on the assignment requirements. Make sure to submit before the deadline.

Where can I view my grades?

Your grades are available in the "Academic Reports" section on the dashboard. You can view grades for individual assignments, course grades, and your overall GPA. Instructors typically post grades within 7-10 days after assignment due dates.
