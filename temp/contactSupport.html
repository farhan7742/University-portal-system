Common Issues & Solutions

Before contacting support, check if your issue is addressed in our FAQs:

How long does it take to get a response from support?

Response times vary based on issue urgency: Critical issues (within 2 hours), Urgent problems (within 4 hours), Technical issues (within 24 hours), General questions (within 48 hours).

What information should I include in my support request?

Include your user ID, detailed description of the issue, steps to reproduce, error messages, screenshots, and what you've already tried to resolve the issue.
